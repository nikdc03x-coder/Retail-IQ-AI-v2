## 2. Stage-by-Stage Data Lifecycle

### Stage 1: Raw Ingestion & Schema Enforcement
- **Input:** `train.csv` (421,570 rows), `features.csv` (8,190 rows), `stores.csv` (45 rows).
- **Checks:** Explicit schema dtypes, date parsing (`YYYY-MM-DD`), non-emptiness, primary key uniqueness `(Store, Dept, Date)`.
- **Join Operation:** Left-join `train` with `features` on `(Store, Date)` and `stores` on `Store`.
- **Output:** `data/processed/analytical_dataset.parquet` (421,570 rows, 20 columns, 3.4 MB). Zero dropped rows. 100% agreement on `IsHoliday`.

### Stage 2: Data Quality & Profiling
- **Calculations:** Column-wise statistics, missingness classification, returns audit ($-\$88,161.56$ across 1,285 rows), store-department continuity (2,660 complete pairs).
- **Reports:** `reports/data_quality/` (5 CSVs + 1 JSON).

### Stage 3: Business Analytics & Aggregations
- **Hierarchical Rollup:** Aggregation of department sales to store-week level ($N = 6,435$ rows across 45 stores $\times$ 143 weeks).
- **Network Aggregations:** Total network weekly sales ($N = 143$ dates), store cumulative rankings, department market shares, holiday event comparison (Thanksgiving $+41.34\%$, Christmas $-2.59\%$).
- **Reports:** `reports/business_analytics/` (7 CSVs).

### Stage 4: Sales Forecasting & Feature Engineering
- **Feature Construction:**
  - Autoregressive lags: `lag_1`, `lag_2`, `lag_4`, `lag_52` (strictly shifted by $+1$ week to avoid leakage).
  - Rolling statistics: `rolling_mean_4`, `rolling_std_4`, `rolling_mean_8` (backward-looking).
  - Calendar features: `Month`, `WeekOfYear`, `sin_week`, `cos_week`, `is_holiday_num`.
  - Store metadata: `Size`, `Type_A`, `Type_B`, `Type_C`.
  - Macroeconomic indices: `Temperature`, `Fuel_Price`, `CPI`, `Unemployment`.
- **Temporal Splitting:**
  - Training: 2011-02-11 to 2011-12-30 (2,115 store-weeks)
  - Validation: 2012-01-06 to 2012-05-25 (945 store-weeks)
  - Held-Out Test: 2012-06-01 to 2012-10-26 (990 store-weeks, 22 weeks $\times$ 45 stores)
- **Model Training:** Previous-Week Naive, Seasonal Naive (52-wk), Ridge Regressor, Random Forest, XGBoost.
- **Reports & Models:** `models/` (3 joblib files), `reports/sales_forecasting/` (4 files).

### Stage 5: Forecast Reliability & Error Diagnostics
- **Calculations:** Store-level WAPE, MAE, RMSE; win rates against Naive baseline; holiday error shifts ($+20.8\%$ WAPE increase); 4-fold expanding window backtests.
- **Reports:** `reports/forecast_reliability/` (5 CSVs + 1 JSON).

### Stage 6: Anomaly Detection & Investigation Queue
- **Calculations:**
  - Statistical detector: Standardized residual $Z = (e_i - \mu) / \sigma$, threshold $|Z| > 3.0$.
  - Multivariate detector: Isolation Forest on residuals, actual sales, fuel price, and temperature ($2\%$ contamination).
  - Agreement matrix: Unanimous consensus flags ($N = 14$), statistical-only ($N = 3$), isolation-only ($N = 6$).
  - Priority scoring: Multi-criteria score combining residual magnitude, Z-score, and isolation score.
- **Reports:** `reports/anomaly_detection/` (5 CSVs + 1 JSON).

### Stage 7: Explainable AI (TreeSHAP)
- **Calculations:** TreeSHAP explainer on champion XGBoost model; global mean absolute SHAP in dollars and relative %; local waterfall additive decomposition; mathematical verification of the efficiency axiom $f(x) = \phi_0 + \sum \phi_i$.
- **Reports:** `reports/explainable_ai/` (4 CSVs + 1 JSON).

## 3. End-to-End Lineage & Reproducibility
Every downstream metric in the dashboard and AI assistant maps directly to an explicit upstream pipeline script and saved report file:
- `app.py` and `p08_assistant.py` execute zero training or heavy data transformation at runtime.
- Rerunning `python scripts/run_all_pipelines.py` completely regenerates all artifacts deterministically from raw data in under 40 seconds.
